Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Overview

Gio's Auto Glass respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information you provide

When you submit our quote form, we collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name, phone number, service needs, vehicle details, and message. We use this information only to respond to your request, provide an estimate, schedule service, and communicate about your appointment.

Information collected automatically

This website may receive basic technical information from your browser, such as device type and pages viewed. We do not use this site to sell personal information. Embedded Google Maps and links to social networks may collect information under their own policies when you interact with them.

Netlify form processing

Quote submissions are processed and stored by Netlify on behalf of Gio's Auto Glass. Access is limited to handling customer inquiries and service requests. Netlify may process data in accordance with its own privacy terms.

Retention and security

We retain inquiry information only as long as reasonably necessary for customer service, business records, legal, or safety purposes. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no online transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask what personal information we have about you, request correction, or ask us to delete information when legally and operationally possible. Contact giosautoglass@gmail.com.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy when our practices change. Questions about this policy can be sent to Gio's Auto Glass, Tulsa, Oklahoma, at giosautoglass@gmail.com or (918) 816-6883.
